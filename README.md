# Heart Anatomy for Catheterization

An interactive, animated presentation on the anatomy of the heart, built as Part 1 of a cardiac catheterization seminar.

**Created by Khaled Odeh, The Hashemite University**

## Contents (10 sections, about 12 minutes)
1. Learning objectives
2. The heart at a glance: position, size, surfaces, borders
3. Chambers & valves
4. Oxygenated vs deoxygenated blood flow (pulmonary & systemic circuits)
5. Wall layers & pericardium
6. Coronary arteries: the "crown", origins and key terms
7. Right coronary artery and its branches
8. Left coronary artery and its branches
9. Coronary circulation: territories, venous return, anastomoses
10. Applied anatomy: to the cath lab

## How to present
- Open `index.html` in any modern browser (works offline).
- **Desktop:** arrow keys, Space, or a presentation clicker. Press **F** for full screen.
- **Tablet / phone:** tap to start, then swipe left/right or use the arrow buttons.

Content is based on the course book *Anatomy of Heart*.
