## Using the presentation
- Arrow keys, Space, Page Up/Down or a presentation clicker move between slides; swipe on a phone or tablet.
- The bar at the bottom is grouped by presenter; click a part name to jump to it. On the objectives slide, click a part card to jump there.
- Click any photo or illustration to enlarge it; use ← → to move between figures, Esc to close.
- `F` toggles full screen. The timer restarts when a new presenter's part begins.

Works on desktop, tablet and mobile. Everything is in one self-contained `index.html` file.
